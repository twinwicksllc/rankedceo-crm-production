## What shipped

**#299 — `feat(waas): remove fabricated reviews; add owner-entered real
reviews (B1)`.** Two commits, 18 files, +1289/−270.

- `ReviewNFCSection` renders only owner-entered data. With no reviews, no
  Google rating and no Google URL it renders nothing. The rating badge needs
  both a rating and a count and is never averaged from the curated reviews.
- The "Write a Google Review" button and "See all reviews on Google" link use
  the tenant's real `brand_config.social.google` URL and only if it is
  http(s).
- Owner entry in the client editor: six fixed review slots (name, 1–5 stars,
  text, optional date) plus Google rating and review count. Server-side
  validation in `validateReviewValue` stops a direct action call storing a
  9-star rating, an over-length review or a half-formed badge.
- Generators and AI output can no longer write review data
  (`stripUnverifiedReviewData` in both `sanitizeSections`; the prompt forbids
  it). The website builder lost its fake "Verified Customer" testimonial.
- **Copilot review (2 high, 2 medium) was fixed before merge, in `80d998b`:**
  1. Legacy AI-written reviews could render as owner-entered. Owner keys are
     now prefixed `owner*` (`ownerReviews`, `ownerGoogleRating`,
     `ownerGoogleReviewCount`); the old unprefixed keys are ignored on read
     and stripped on write. No migration was needed (DEC-0013 respected).
  2. AI regeneration of the Reviews section could fabricate testimonials.
     `reviews` is removed from the regeneration allow-list on the server and
     in the editor UI, driven by one shared module,
     `lib/waas/client-edit/regeneratable-sections.ts`.
  3. Decimal ratings were rounded. Stars now fill fractionally and the
     accessible label carries the exact value ("4.8 out of 5 stars").
  4. The Google link was hidden for aggregate-only ratings. It now shows
     whenever a valid URL exists.
- A bug my own tests caught: the generic array-item rule in `validateEditPath`
  rejected `ownerReviews[M].*` before the review rule ran. The review block
  now comes first.

## Behavior change to expect on live sites

Tenants lose the fake review cards, badge and NFC link at deploy, and the
Reviews section disappears until an owner adds real reviews or a Google URL.
The header and footer "Reviews" links point at `#reviews`; they do nothing
while the section is hidden. Whether tenants are told is Tommy's call.

## Decisions recorded

- Owner-entered review data uses the `owner*` key prefix. Unprefixed review
  keys are legacy AI output and are never rendered.
- AI may not generate or regenerate anything in the Reviews section beyond its
  eyebrow, headline and subheadline copy, and regeneration is off for it.
- `B4_B3_UNIFIED_BUILDER_PLAN.md` says the Reviews section is not offered in
  the "add a section" library until real reviews can be entered. They now can,
  so that condition is met. Whether to offer it is a decision for B3 PR 4.
